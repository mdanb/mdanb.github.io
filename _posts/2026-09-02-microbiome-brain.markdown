We investigate how gut microbial composition and function relate to structural brain MRI measures in Hispanic and Latino adults, focusing on white matter hyperintensity, hippocampal, and total gray matter volumes. Our findings identify taxonomic and functional associations that support a potential role for the gut microbiome in brain structure and health.
